## 1. Problem, value and current evidence

The representative anonymous dossier now works from Request through ordinary
Project closure. A closer still cannot distinguish a completed consulting
obligation from an ordinary Task marked done or cancelled. A missing result or a
cancelled contractual activity must not silently become fulfilled work.

The desired additional value is a visible explanation of what remains, explicit
review of deviations and one attributable consulting-completion basis. Success
means no governed mission completes with an unresolved required obligation, while
ordinary Projects keep their approved behavior.

The [merged-main milestone proof](https://github.com/Prisma-Prim/PRISMA-PRIM-Suite/blob/5355efd9e10e498675852552f382770e3ce1add9/report.md)
contains one passing public REST case with two API restarts and two actual App
cases at 1440×900/390×844. All thirteen results/PDFs, ordinary closure and closed
dossier reopening passed. Initial commercial setup and thirteen result entries
used public REST fixtures; this is not proof of manual UI entry of every result,
actual performed work, real content approval or operational tenant readiness.
No rerun or new production validation is claimed by this documentation proposal.

## 2. The three decisions requested

Everything below is a recommendation until these decisions are approved.

### A. Required work, evidence and progress

**Recommend:** for a deliberately adopted thirteen-activity mission profile,
every included activity is required initially. A required activity needs its same
copied Task done and a current declared result referencing an exact frozen,
independently readable mission PDF. A done Task without that result remains a
missing obligation; a cancelled required Task remains unfulfilled.
A single exact PDF Version may support several activities when each Task's
declaration explains its result; thirteen distinct Documents are not required.

Show all thirteen activities and their gaps. Permit parallel work and an explicit
next action chosen by the responsible actor; activity order is presentation,
not a new enforced dependency graph. This deliberately narrows D5's first outcome
to activity/obligation progress. Generalized stage transitions wait for their own
approved mapping; the existing pilot stage-advance refusal is unchanged.

**Consequence to approve:** the adopted profile is stricter than ordinary Task
completion. No new standalone-Task or unenrolled-Project rule follows.

### B. Non-applicability and exceptions

**Recommend:** a coordinator may propose that one activity is not applicable,
but only the explicitly authorized mission approver may accept it. Record the
activity, concrete reason, scope/agreement basis, proposing actor, approving
actor and time. A pending/rejected request changes no obligation. Approval is a
visible exclusion, never fulfilled work or an implicit Contract amendment.

An activity that is still due stays required/blocked, with a responsible person,
next action and due date; a delay is not non-applicability. Cancelling a Task,
removing a Document or clearing a result never grants an exception. Restoring
applicability appends a new attributed decision and can block completion again.
No general waiver allowing completion with still-unfulfilled required work is
proposed. Contractual scope changes need their own valid recorded authority;
this UI cannot manufacture agreement by the parties.

**Consequence to approve:** the first-tenant approver is Iurie through explicit
application assignments; technical access and Project responsibility are not
delegation. No real grant is created by this decision.

### C. Completion of consulting, delivery and certification

**Recommend:** a governed mission completes when its included consulting work
and delivery are reviewed by the authorized closer: all required activities have
their declared result evidence, every exclusion is approved, no ordinary Task is
open, and a literal completion/handover basis identifies what was delivered and
how the Client's receipt/acceptance was established or reported.

The closer reviews one complete evidence set and records one explicit decision;
thirteen additional approval clicks are not required. This is an attributed human
acceptance of the consulting outcome, not automated verification of PDF content,
signatures, payment, certification or legal effect. A contradictory or uncertain
delivery/acceptance basis blocks that completion; it is not converted to acceptance
by elapsed time. An existing frozen result PDF may substantiate handover when
appropriate; a new financial Document family is not introduced here.

External certification is a separately evidenced outcome. A support-only mission
may complete after the agreed support and handover without claiming a certificate
was obtained. An explicit engagement to obtain certification remains unresolved
until its required outcome or a separately justified scope decision is recorded.
The annex's intended certification result is retained visibly; payment provisions
alone do not decide its fulfillment. Pending clarification stays blocked rather
than being classified automatically as support-only.

**Consequence to approve:** operational consulting completion and external
certification are distinct, with exact recorded scope and evidence. Broader
financial settlement, assurance and legal-acceptance policy remains outside this
bounded completion record.

## 4. User stories

1. As a coordinator, I inspect all thirteen preserved activities and actual Task
   identities without inferring them from mutable titles.
2. As a mission approver, I deliberately adopt one identified policy Version for
   an eligible open Project and inspect its stricter completion consequences.
3. As a user of an ordinary Project, I retain its existing completion behavior.
4. As a collaborator, I see done-with-result, missing-result, open and cancelled
   work without a claim that my declaration verified the work.
5. As a responsible actor with coordination authority, I record the next action,
   responsible person and due date without imposing a fictional sequential stage.
6. As a coordinator, I propose non-applicability with a concrete scope basis.
7. As an approver, I accept or reject that exact proposal and preserve its history.
8. As a reader, I distinguish an approved exclusion from a completed obligation.
9. As an approver, I restore applicability without erasing the prior decision.
10. As a closer, I see every blocker before choosing completion and cannot bypass
    a required cancelled Task or missing result through the old completion route.
11. As a closer, I inspect one exact evidence set and record a handover basis in
    one deliberate completion act without approving thirteen rows separately.
12. As a reader, I reopen the preserved closure basis after Document succession,
    result history changes, access revocation or Client identity consolidation.
13. As a caller after an uncertain response, I retry the identical intent/key and
    receive its one original outcome under current read/write authority.
14. As a user changing Company, identity, locale or frame, I keep only the work
    whose consumed authority still applies and receive no held old-scope reply.
15. As an administrator, I revoke mission approval without destroying separately
    authorized Project, Task, result or PDF reads.
16. As an auditor of product behavior, I distinguish consulting completion,
    cancellation, Start Exception, signature declaration and certification.

## 5. Concepts, states and corrections

These are proposed terms, not additions to the approved glossary yet.

- **Mission policy adoption:** an immutable link from an existing exact copied
  plan and its thirteen Task identities to one identified obligation policy
  Version. No Project, Task, result, source row or Contract is recreated/rewritten.
- **Activity applicability:** required initially; an approved non-applicability
  decision excludes that row from required work while keeping it visible.
- **Activity readiness:** open work, required Task cancelled, missing result,
  result/evidence unavailable, submitted result, or approved exclusion. Submitted
  is not verified or automatically accepted. Loss of read authority is unavailable,
  not missing, and cannot disclose the protected reason or metadata.
- **Mission completion basis:** the immutable actor/time, policy/source/Task
  identities, observed applicability decisions and exact result/PDF references
  reviewed by the closer, with consulting/handover declarations and their basis.

Only an open Project with the complete recognized thirteen-row snapshot may
adopt the policy; closed Projects cannot be retrofitted. Existing v1 plans remain
readable and usable without adoption. Later adoption cannot be undone to evade
completion guards; changing a policy or required scope uses a separately approved
version/decision path, never an in-place edit. Unknown/missing mapping refuses
adoption. Task title edits, extra Tasks, result correction or Document succession
never change the source mapping automatically.

An exclusion proposal has pending/approved/rejected states against its observed
applicability revision. Decisions are immutable. Reclassification records a new
reasoned decision with a predecessor; concurrent or stale decisions conflict.
A result correction or Task reopening before closure invalidates readiness for
the observed set; the closer must reload/review the new set. Extra Tasks retain
the ordinary no-open-Task completion gate but do not become source obligations.

Project lifecycle still owns activate/hold/resume/complete/cancel. Governance adds
a completion precondition only to adopted missions; it changes no lifecycle
status or stage. Cancellation remains a different, reasoned terminal outcome
under the ordinary no-open-Task gate, retaining unfulfilled obligations visibly.
It never records successful mission completion. Closed Projects stay terminal;
historical reopening means viewing the dossier, not resuming execution.

## 6. Commands, history and invariants

Commands carry observed policy/adoption/applicability/lifecycle and consumed
Task/result revisions, a stable actor/Workspace idempotency key and only the
explicit intent. The server establishes actor, time, scope and exact references.
Client booleans or counts cannot attest fulfillment. Bounds, normalization and
closed payload shapes follow the existing command discipline and are fixed in
each implementation issue; no caller-selected SQL, rule language or arbitrary
workflow graph is accepted.

Use the original Client/relationship and Project serialization order. Completion
rechecks the entire exact obligation set, current authority and no-open-Task gate
in the same transaction as lifecycle change, immutable reviewed snapshot, receipt,
minimal Activity event and Client operational-use fence. Every existing completion
entry point must enforce adoption; no legacy endpoint bypass is permitted.

Completion versus Task reopen/cancel/edit, result correction, policy decision,
consumed Document qualification/authority change, access revocation, Client
archive/union and competing lifecycle change must have one serializable outcome. A changed evidence set refuses the
stale confirmation, rather than reviewing whatever is latest at commit time.
An unrelated field change or a later official designation does not invalidate an
unchanged eligible historical PDF reference or add authority it did not consume.
No failed command leaves a partial approval, lifecycle change, receipt or event.
The database independently constrains scoped references, unique adoption,
immutable decisions and coupled completion evidence. PostgreSQL defense does not
replace authorization or human review.

Exact retries recheck live consumed authority and target visibility, then return
the conserved receipt before rejecting its old observed revision. Changed-key
reuse conflicts. Replays do not duplicate history, Project/Task records or events.
An earlier read cannot replace confirmed completion; uncertainty conserves the
same intent/key. Missing capability or exact-scope changes retire only the
affected protected intent, preserving independent result/PDF/navigation owners.

Activity Log owns attributable adoption, applicability decisions, next-action
changes and reviewed closure. Event metadata stays minimal; reasons, declarations
and PDF identities remain in their separately authorized owning records. No
customer prose, evidence bytes or inaccessible Company identity enters generic
Activity metadata.

## 7. Documents, interface and failure behavior

Document Registry/§27 remain the source of truth for exact clean frozen PDF
identity, official/formerly official status and historical access. A later
designation cannot retarget a reviewed reference. Record the reviewed evidence
Version/representation/checksum through the Document-owned interface; never use
a bare URL, mutable title or latest-Version selector as proof.

The Project exposes its thirteen-row obligation progress, explanatory blockers,
current next action, applicability history and completion preview in the existing
accepted Prisme frame. All supported interface locales use shared copy; literal
source/declarations stay unchanged. Use explicit keyboard/focus handling and
desktop/narrow layouts. Loading, no adopted policy, denied, unavailable, stale,
conflicting, uncertain, confirmed and closed-history states remain distinct.
No hidden-control bypass, silent zero count or empty-success interpretation.

An actor without independent result/content authority may retain their authorized
Project/Task context, but sees unavailable review data and cannot approve it.
Scope/cursor/history/download refusal reveals no foreign Company/Workspace facts.
External storage/scanner failure follows the delivered Document boundary; this
proposal adds no external provider, public signed URL or background integration.

## 8. Compatibility, migration and operational limits

No backfill infers obligations, applicability, performed work or permission from
existing Projects/Tasks, roles or archived dossiers. No active or closed Project
is silently enrolled. New tables/capabilities are additive with reviewed migration;
upgrade/repeat-upgrade proof conserves old identities, states, exact PDFs,
receipts, grants and history. Ordinary Task/result/Project APIs retain behavior
outside explicitly adopted missions.

The first profile is bounded to thirteen source activities; reads/commands bound
extra input and history through the existing pagination conventions. No unbounded
tree traversal, document-wide content scan, AI decision or external certification
lookup belongs in a completion transaction. No new dependency/provider is implied.
Recovery and retention inherit the platform and exact-Document owners.

## 9. Test seams and acceptance

Reuse real PostgreSQL public REST for scope, immutable mapping, revisions,
permissions, exact replay, coupled rollback and native SQL constraints. Existing
Project lifecycle, copied-plan, Task-result, Document and reported-signing tests
are prior art. Extend actual App acceptance at 1440×900/390×844 for one visible
complete adopted mission and the relevant refusals, without weakening old tests.

The approved implementation contract must prove at least:

1. One existing Project/thirteen Tasks is adopted explicitly, with no duplicate
   records, no source rewrite and no grant widening; ordinary Projects stay usable.
2. A required done Task without a result, cancelled Task, open Task or uncertain
   scope/evidence blocks governed completion through every public entry point.
3. A pending/rejected exclusion does not satisfy readiness; an approved justified
   exclusion is visible and distinct from fulfillment, and restoring required
   status blocks again until properly satisfied.
4. Thirteen current exact result/PDF references and a reviewed consulting/handover
   basis permit one completion by the authorized closer without certifying work.
   Reusing one eligible exact PDF for several explicitly declared results is valid;
   no artificial one-Document-per-activity rule is introduced.
5. A later Task/result/applicability change invalidates the observed confirmation;
   both serial orders and competing same/distinct keys preserve one valid result.
6. Two API restarts conserve adoption, approvals, receipts, reviewed closure and
   all exact result/PDF bytes; closed history is readable without resuming work.
7. Foreign Company/Workspace, unknown captures, revoked approval and independently
   denied evidence fail without metadata leaks; unrelated reads/downloads survive.
8. Genuine previous-schema upgrade and repeat upgrade preserve old public facts;
   forged cross-scope, missing-coupled and mutable evidence is refused natively.
9. Actual App demonstrates blockers, proposed/approved exception, uncertain retry,
   exact review and closed-dossier navigation; locale/frame/lifetime/access cases
   retain literal input and independent ownership.
10. No certificate, verified NC closure, payment/financial settlement, legal
    acceptance or source Contract amendment follows from this mission record.

Full verification and bounded review follow the repository execution policy after
approved vertical issues exist. This Draft requires documentation verification;
runtime tests listed here are future requirements, not executed proof.

## 10. Exclusions and deliberately separate decisions

- Universal workflow/model editors, automatic stage transitions, a mapping from
  eighteen Offer stages to thirteen Contract rows, Gantt and arbitrary dependencies.
- Real tenant activation/content approval, grants, provider setup or deployment.
- Legal clause selection, automatic tacit acceptance, Contract amendments,
  official numbering, payment accounting or actual profitability.
- Accreditation, Certificate issuance, automatic NC resolution or Training
  credential validation. Intended result wording grants none of these.
- Automatic resolution/alerts for the existing Start Exception, kickoff generation,
  changes to the approved seven-item activation declaration or its immutable record.
- Reopening terminal Projects, deleting historical evidence, changing original
  Company/Client identity or retargeting stored result sources.
- A general exception permitting completed status despite remaining required work.
