### 22.1 Case-specific acceptance and the C1 prerequisite

Evidence E02 describes one real case: after transmission of the Offer, the Client supplied details for the Contract, then signed the Contract and paid. Iurie considers acceptance confirmed by explicit progression to the Contract and its signature. E07 p. 5 visibly contains both parties' signature areas. These statements do not establish a universal policy, verified payment, signatory mandate, cryptographic validity or a distinct pre-signature Client acceptance record.

The current illustrative route records a simulated acceptance in O6 before C1 creates a Contract from an Accepted Offer (sections 18.15 and 19.17). Applying E02 automatically would create a circular prerequisite: the signature that confirms acceptance would belong to a Contract that cannot yet be created. The real-use path therefore needs a separate explicit product decision; E02 does not silently waive C1's gate.

| Unresolved real-use policy option                                        | Consequence                                                                                                                            | Decision/evidence still needed                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Retain separate acceptance before Contract creation                      | Preserve O6 → C1; capture an explicit Client acceptance before preparation                                                             | Which pre-signature evidence suffices and who confirms it; legal details alone are not established acceptance                                          |
| Allow governed Contract preparation while Offer response remains pending | Distinguish preparation intent from acceptance; later signature may support a separately recorded acceptance of an exact Offer Version | Eligible states, exact version/concordance rules, actors, evidence and atomic/retry behavior; resolve unsigned, changed, rejected and cancelled drafts |

Section 22.4 selects the bounded preparation option for an illustrative Contract, with exact Offer source per Contract Version and an explicit opt-in write capability. It does not select a universal real acceptance, signing or legal-effect policy. Preparation creates no acceptance; approval/signature still require recorded acceptance of the exact source. The existing C1 accepted-Offer route remains available and the direct origin is not a workaround for a pending Offer. Received files do not become imported Contracts.

### 22.4 Bounded pending-Offer Contract preparation (#821)

**Status:** Approved by delegated product disposition, 2026-10-23, after ordered independent Standards and Spec review under #821; runtime delivery is tracked in #823. The owner's instruction to proceed with #820 D1 authorizes this bounded product synthesis over E02's known sequence. It is not new Iurie evidence or approval of real prices, clauses, signing or external use.

**Outcome and identity.** An explicitly authorized preparer selects the exact approved/transmitted Offer Version with pending Client response, creates an illustrative Contract and reopens its complete draft Version. Use the existing Contract/Contract Version identities, not a second preparation aggregate. The immutable Contract origin is `OFFER_PREPARATION`; the initial source remains attributable. The Contract belongs to the same Company, Opportunity and original Client throughout. It occupies the existing main-Contract slot; recovery reuses that Contract through a new Version rather than creating duplicates. Precontracts, Addenda and direct origins are not new preparation options in D1.

Every Contract Version under this origin captures its own exact source Offer Version. A later explicit revision may select an eligible successor Version of the same Offer/Opportunity/original Client, preserving earlier source/content/representation/history. Initial source metadata on the Contract never rewrites Version-level provenance. Existing `ACCEPTED_OFFER` Contracts retain their fixed-source behavior and all old representations and checks. Root or Version sources never transfer to another Opportunity, Client or named legal entity. Recovery covers same-party source succession; changing the contracting party requires a separately approved remediation outside D1, never a hidden DIRECT fallback or in-place identity transfer.

**Permission.** Authorization section8 owns an independent opt-in Company Contract Early Preparation v1 capability, in addition to ordinary captured Contract preparation, exact source Offer/Opportunity read and the existing Company Client identity-read authority of the Client recipient-copy boundary. Old assignments do not expand. Only early-origin creation and revision require the extra capability. Existing Contract readers read these Contracts and exact historical Versions under the ordinary Contract/Opportunity boundary, without acquiring Offer or pricing authority. Existing approvers retain ordinary approval/signature rights, subject to the acceptance/check gates below; preparing a draft confers none. Unknown/null/revoked captured versions and foreign contexts fail closed.

**New source eligibility.** Initial creation requires an `OPEN` Opportunity, no existing main Contract, and its current non-superseded same-Company Offer Version: internally `APPROVED`, transmitted, response `PENDING`, with validity not elapsed at the fenced server instant. Original Client and Company relationship must be active and the named party available through the existing Client public boundary. New revision requires an unexecuted early-origin Contract and observed Contract revision. It may explicitly select a current eligible pending source under those same conditions, or an exact current Accepted Offer under the existing accepted-source rules. A successor must also retain the Contract's fixed named Client Legal Entity under section19.2 PC7: the same primary entity or the same additional-entity identifier. A different party refuses before any write; matching only the shared Client is insufficient. Scalar identity corrections retain the existing identity-warning rules. An accepted source does not retroactively change the Contract's origin. The ordinary accepted-source Opportunity rules remain unchanged.

Internal pending/rejection, missing transmission, Client refusal, recorded/elapsed pending expiry, supersession, wrong Company/Opportunity/Client or lost required authority prevent a new source binding. The accepted source case uses its established accepted-source semantics rather than inventing a new expiry rule. Existing-kind refusal order remains documented by section19; a caller cannot choose DIRECT or PRECONTRACT to bypass early-source authority. Recheck/fence authority, source, party and Contract/head consistently with the owning modules. A source invalidation committed first prevents the new mutation.

**Content and publication.** Inputs are selected source Offer Version UUID, the existing nullable proposed start/end dates and literal special conditions (maximum4,000 characters), plus observed Contract revision for a successor. Use existing Contract validation, including calendar order, NUL refusal and unknown-field refusal. Server fixes Company, Opportunity, original Client, recipient identity copied from the exact source, Pricing Snapshot reference, identities, positive Version number, actor/time and source provenance. It copies the source's scope/commercial conditions without price re-entry or recalculation. Party availability/current-identity differences follow the existing public Client and quality boundaries, not caller-controlled identity.

Use a server-fixed `CONTRACT_PREPARATION_ILLUSTRATIVE` Template v1 whose complete internal representation names the exact source Offer Version without describing it as accepted. It preserves the existing Contract fields and provisional/draft markings, rendering party, immutable Snapshot amount/conditions and proposed terms. No received clauses or actual values enter Git. Superseded Versions retain their exact content and representation; later source or Client changes never rewrite them. This is a usable internal draft, not an official/operational model, transmission, signature, acceptance or payment.

**Acceptance, Quality Checks and execution.** Early-origin runs bind `CONTRACT_QUALITY_ILLUSTRATIVE` v3. They retain the existing full adjusted-Snapshot coherence and identity/term/provisional checks, use `PRICE_MATCHES_SOURCE_OFFER` and `CONDITIONS_MATCH_SOURCE_OFFER` for exact-source concordance, and add `SOURCE_OFFER_ACCEPTED` as an ERROR unless the exact bound source remains current and has recorded Client acceptance. While pending, checks may explain this error, but approval and signature cannot pass. Rejection/supersession/expiry before acceptance cannot be acknowledged away as warnings. Existing v1/v2 runs and ordinary origin bindings remain unchanged; new v3 checks never relabel historical runs.

After explicit ordinary Client acceptance of the exact source, the same draft may pass fresh v3 checks, receive the independent ordinary approval and record the existing simulated signatures. If the source changes instead, explicitly create a new Contract Version against its eligible successor and review that Version; preserve the old one. Ordinary rejection recovery remains a new Version. Executed Versions remain immutable; Addenda and existing authorized Project links use their ordinary existing rules. This maintains one coherent Contract workflow without auto-acceptance, an alternative acceptance command or an execution bypass. Actual external-signature/acceptance evidence remains #820 D10.

**Receipts and atomicity.** The actor-scoped key binds normalized command/Opportunity/Contract, selected exact source, expected revision and literal terms. Validate once at the public owner. Resolve exact committed receipts after live early-preparation, ordinary Contract/Opportunity/source-read and Company Client identity-read prerequisites, before stale revision, new-source eligibility or active-original checks. Later source acceptance/refusal/expiry/supersession, Opportunity closure or Client supersession cannot erase an authorized committed result; current authority loss still denies. Changed intent under a key conflicts. A stale uncommitted revision requires explicit reload and a new deliberate intent/key.

Contract/Version/source binding, head/revision, reference allocation on initial creation only, receipt, original-Client operational-use fence and minimal existing Contract events commit atomically. Denied, invalid, stale, failed and replayed commands create no extra Version/reference/event/fence. Identical concurrent keys converge; distinct keys against one expected revision produce one successor and a conflict. Source response/version, main Contract creation, revocation/suspension and Client relationship/union changes serialize through existing public owners. Final-write/event/response-construction failure rolls back the complete command. Additive migration preserves all old identities, source bindings, grants, references, history and receipts, assigning no new authority or prepared Contract retroactively.

**Reads, history and UI.** Extend the existing exact Contract Version and stable paginated history owners, retaining ordinary authorization and no new global list/count/search. For an early origin, each view exposes that Version's own source and separately labelled current eligibility; it never substitutes the root's initial source for a successor or invents acceptance when current source facts are unavailable. Copied Contract content remains readable under ordinary Contract rights even if independent Offer rights are later lost. A fresh source selection/publication requires the full separate write prerequisites.

In the existing accepted Prisme Opportunity/Offer/Contract journey, show a clear early-preparation action only under its captured write capability. Choose the current eligible Offer Version by visible label, never UUID entry. The full saved draft, pending source/check refusal, previous Versions, selected source and explicit successor/reload flow remain clear. An existing early Contract is opened and revised, never replaced by a duplicate C1 create. Ordinary accepted/direct/Precontract/Addendum controls retain their contracts. Source unavailable/rejected/superseded states provide a safe explanation and eligible reselection, not fake acceptance or a hidden DIRECT fallback.

Preserve literal text, calendar values, selected Version and exact uncertain body/key across locale changes with no locale-only request/save. Company, Opportunity, principal/session/membership and captured required authority changes clear protected drafts/data, cancel owned requests and suppress late replies; ordinary read-only Contract history may remain only under its independent current read owner. Source changes retire the selected write candidate until explicit reselection. Confirmed save stays confirmed after failed refresh; uncertain save permits explicit unchanged retry. Cover loading/empty/denied/error/stale/uncertain/success, keyboard/focus and desktop1440/narrow390 using existing four-locale controls. This is one business feature, not a localization rollout.

**Acceptance and proof.** The later runtime child completes explicit grant/revoke administration and the complete schema/API/UI/history flow. Its registered real App/PostgreSQL journey creates an approved/transmitted pending Offer, grants early preparation, creates/reloads the full draft, shows acceptance blocking approval/signature, revises proposed terms, changes to an eligible successor Offer Version without losing original content, explicitly accepts the exact current source and completes the existing illustrative check/approval/signature path. Existing Project linking remains eligible only after execution. No preparation command changes Client response or creates actual signing/payment evidence.

Public REST proof covers malformed fields/calendar/limits, every source eligibility/refusal including a successor naming another legal entity of the same Client, ordinary versus early captured authority and revocation, foreign scope/identifiers, readable history after source/Client change, exact receipts after later source changes, changed intent, deterministic concurrent key/revision and source/authority/Client invalidation, complete rollback, restart and old-schema migration preservation. Exact Version history, representations, v1/v2 behavior and all ordinary origins must remain valid. Registered App proof covers source labels/reselection, full draft/history, context/authority retirement, unchanged retry, confirmed-save/failed-refresh separation, literal values and keyboard/fit at both widths. Distinguish synthetic fixtures, disposable PostgreSQL and real provider evidence. Applicable review precedes final current-main integration verification/CI under the standing bounded procedure.
