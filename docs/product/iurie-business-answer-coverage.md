## 1. Reading rules

Two independent assessments are preserved:

- **Evidence authority** distinguishes a direct or faithfully paraphrased Iurie response, an agent-framed proposition explicitly accepted by Iurie, and a decision delegated to Dan or the agents.
- Iurie's interview agent is an intermediary: it may explain, reformulate and request examples. Iurie remains the authority on business reality and desired needs.
- An agent formulation accepted by Iurie is evidence only for the scope actually stated. An agent interpretation or recommendation without explicit Iurie acceptance is never a business requirement.
- A point delegated by Iurie to Dan or the agents is a pending product or technical decision, not confirmed business behaviour.
- **Source-declared status** reproduces the interview comment's own `Resolved`, `Partially resolved` or other status. It describes the source author's assessment.
- **Semantic coverage** compares what was actually asked and answered with every component of the official question.
- **Full** means every material component of the official question is answered by the attributed Iurie response.
- **Partial** means the evidence answers only a narrower reformulation, an example, one component, or a compound proposal while material components of the official wording remain not asked or deferred.
- A response that fully answers the question actually asked is not called insufficient. The matrix preserves it and labels the remaining official components **not asked / deferred remainder**.
- **Not covered** means an official ID was attached, but the actual question answers a materially different question.
- **Not mapped** means no atomic source section is attached to the official ID. It does not mean the subject was never discussed.
- A source-declared `Resolved` never overrides the semantic audit.
- The actual-question column is mandatory context when the answer is a bare `Da` or another short confirmation.
- Later interview sessions switched to an independent numerical sequence. Those contributions are inventoried separately and must not be silently equated with official question-bank IDs.

## 9. Decision sufficiency and follow-up protocol

Formal bank completeness and the amount of evidence actually needed for a product decision are different measures. Do not mechanically re-ask every partial question.

Classify each gap as one of:

- **Sufficient** — the available answer supports the intended decision without a material missing component.
- **Sufficient with reversible assumption** — a low-risk, reversible product choice may proceed with the assumption and confidence recorded explicitly.
- **Interviewer clarification** — Iurie's agent should clarify wording, distinguish components or request an example, ideally immediately in the same interview.
- **Iurie follow-up required** — ask a short targeted question when the gap affects a business rule, exception, authorization, legal or financial effect, blocking automation or a decision difficult to reverse.
- **Dan/technical decision** — do not ask Iurie to choose architecture, data structures, provider mechanics or another technical/product-design mechanism.

When a material gap is discovered only during consolidation, prepare a short prioritized completion package for Iurie's agent. It must include the initial question/answer and provenance; what is already understood; the exact not-asked/deferred remainder; the concrete business reason; the blocked decision/PRD/issue or risk; one minimal natural Romanian follow-up; neutral examples only if useful; and the closure condition. Avoid redundant or purely technical questions. Make clear that the agent is completing an insufficient prior answer, not restarting the questionnaire.

Future interview comments should use official IDs whenever possible.
