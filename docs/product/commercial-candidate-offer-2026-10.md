- **Status:** Non-canonical candidate — not an approved operational Template

Every token is an empty review slot. Section labels are English technical labels for preparing localized variants; this is not ready-to-send Romanian or Russian wording. Source-derived means the field or section was observed, not that its contents are confirmed or approved. The CSV owns exact provenance and reuse gates. All legal and commercial prose must remain blank until separately approved; no token supplies a runtime default.
